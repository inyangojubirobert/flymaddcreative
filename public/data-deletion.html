Data Deletion

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

This page explains how a One Dream Initiative user can request deletion of specific personal data, what is deleted, what may be retained, and for how long, in addition to the full account deletion option.

Deleting Specific Data Yourself

You can already remove some of your own content directly in the app or website without contacting us:

  • Delete your own support messages sent to our support/AI assistant, from the message thread in the One Dream app or the user dashboard on the website.
  • Delete your own order messages sent to a buyer or seller within a catalogue order chat.

These self-service deletions remove the message content immediately; they do not delete the underlying account, profile, or transaction records.

Requesting Deletion of Other Data

For data you cannot delete yourself (profile fields, uploaded media, votes, withdrawal history, or other account data), email flymaddcreative@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, describing what you want deleted. We aim to respond to and complete verified requests within 30 days.

Request Data Deletion

What Is Deleted

  • Profile information (name, bio, contact details, photos/videos, social links) you ask us to remove.
  • Support and order messages you authored, once you delete them or ask us to.
  • Any other personal data you specifically identify in your request, to the extent it is not needed for the retention reasons below.

What May Be Retained, and Why

Some data is kept even after a deletion request because it is required for legal, security, or payment-related reasons:

  • Payment and transaction records (Paystack payment references, USDT/crypto transaction hashes, withdrawal records) — retained to meet financial, accounting, and legal reporting obligations.
  • Fraud-prevention and security records (e.g. account activity logs tied to votes, payments, or disputes) — retained to detect fraud, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
  • Public blockchain transaction data — controlled by the underlying blockchain network, not by us, and cannot be altered or deleted.
  • Any information we are required to keep by applicable law or regulation.

Retention Period

Retained payment, transaction, and fraud-prevention records are kept for as long as needed to satisfy the legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes above; the system does not currently implement a separate fixed automatic-deletion timer for these records beyond that operational need. See our Privacy Policy for full retention details.
