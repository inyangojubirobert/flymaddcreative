Account Deletion

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

This page explains how a One Dream Initiative participant, merchant, or user can request deletion of their account and associated data from the app and website operated by Flymadd Creative.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send a deletion request from the email address associated with your account to flymaddcreative@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request", including your registered username or email. We aim to confirm and complete verified requests within 30 days.

Request Account Deletion

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile information (name, username, bio, contact details, photos/videos, social links).
  • Your login credentials and sign-in access to the app and website.
  • Support messages, order messages, and other content you personally authored, where technically separable from other users' records.

What May Be Retained

Some information cannot be immediately deleted and may be retained for a period even after your account is deleted, including:

  • Transaction, payment, and withdrawal records (Paystack, USDT/crypto), retained to meet financial, accounting, and legal obligations.
  • Records needed to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.
  • Public blockchain transaction records, which are controlled by their respective networks and cannot be altered or deleted by us.
  • Information we are required to keep by law or regulation.

See our Data Deletion page and Privacy Policy for more detail on retention.

Contact Us

For account deletion or privacy questions, email flymaddcreative@gmail.com.
