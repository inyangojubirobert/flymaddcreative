Listing currencies

Sellers choose the currency of each listing. Naira listings are paid by Paystack in naira and settle in naira. Every other currency is paid in USDT at its US-dollar value, fixed in a checkout quote, and settles in US dollars. Switching listing currencies on also needs protected patches P7 + P8 applied and deployed (docs/protected-patches).
