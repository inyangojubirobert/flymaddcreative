Merchant Program Rules

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Your account

  • One merchant account per email address. Keep your password private; you are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Give accurate details. You can update your name, company and payout wallet from the merchant dashboard.

How you earn

  • Each merchant gets one or more referral links. A participant who registers through your link is linked to you.
  • Paid voting ended on 19 October 2026, so referrals no longer earn tokens from votes.
  • Gift referral rewards are coming soon. Their rate and rules will be published on this page before they start.

Withdrawals

  • The minimum withdrawal is 50 tokens. Withdrawals are paid to the wallet address saved on your account, so check it before you withdraw.
  • Withdrawals are reviewed before payment and may be held while suspected abuse is investigated.

Not allowed

  • Referring yourself, creating fake participants, or paying people only to register through your link.
  • Spam, misleading claims about the One Dream Initiative, or using someone else's name or brand.

Breaking these rules can lead to rewards being cancelled and the account being suspended.

Your data

How we handle your information is explained in the Privacy Policy.
